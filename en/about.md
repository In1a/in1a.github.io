# About

Hi, I'm **Yifan Bao**, an undergraduate computer science student at the University of British Columbia (UBC).

I enjoy algorithms and competitive programming, and I'm interested in parallel computing, mathematics, and computer systems. Beyond technical topics, I'd also like to write about books, everyday life, and thoughts worth remembering. There isn't a single theme to this blog—it's simply a place to keep notes as I go.

Some of my earlier algorithm posts were migrated from [my CNBlogs archive](https://www.cnblogs.com/evenbao), with their original dates and source links preserved.
